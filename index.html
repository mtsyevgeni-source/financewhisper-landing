What it does

Talk to it in plain English, or use exact commands — both work the same way.

Price & percent alerts

"Let me know when Tesla drops below $300" or "alert me if Apple moves 5%." Fires once, then it's done.

Watchlist & portfolio

Track tickers with no alert condition, or log real holdings and see live gain/loss, cost basis included.

Instant quotes & market snapshot

"What's Microsoft trading at?" or "how's the market doing?" — with a short, synthesized news briefing.

Proactive news alerts

A background check screens headlines on tickers you track and only messages you when something is actually likely to move the stock — not every headline.

Daily digest

One message a day: market snapshot, watchlist, portfolio P&L, and your active alert count.

Crypto & forex too

Major coins and currency pairs work everywhere a stock ticker does — just say BTC, ETH, or the name.
